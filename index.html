ABOUT THE PORTAL

Turning research findings into an accessible resource.

This portal presents selected findings from the research study on differentiated instruction strategies represented in teacher education textbooks available at Mabalacat City College.

It is designed to help pre-service teachers, faculty members, and other academic stakeholders explore the instructional strategies identified through systematic content analysis.
