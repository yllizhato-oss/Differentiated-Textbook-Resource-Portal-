Differentiated Content

Ways teachers adjust what learners study according to readiness, interests, and learning needs.

P

Differentiated Process

Different learning activities and approaches that help students understand content.

Different ways learners demonstrate what they know, understand, and can do.

DI Framework

Four Dimensions of Differentiation

The content analysis examines how Differentiated Instruction appears across four major dimensions.

01

Content

What students learn and the knowledge, skills, and concepts presented to them.

02

Process

How learners engage with and make sense of the content.

03

Product

How learners demonstrate their learning and understanding.

04

Learning Environment

The physical, social, and instructional conditions surrounding learning.

Research

About the Study

This study examines the representation of Differentiated Instruction in selected textbooks used in the Bachelor of Elementary Education program.

Through content analysis, the study identifies instructional strategies and determines how they are represented across the four dimensions of Differentiated Instruction.

  • Identify Differentiated Instruction strategies represented in selected textbooks.
  • Categorize identified strategies according to Content, Process, Product, and Learning Environment.
  • Examine similarities and differences across the selected textbooks.
  • Identify areas that are frequently represented, limited, or absent.

Research Process

1

Select eligible BEED textbooks.

2

Review and examine textbook content.

3

Identify and code DI strategies.

4

Categorize strategies according to the DI framework.

5

Compare and interpret findings.

About the Portal

A Resource for Future Educators

This portal presents research-based information about Differentiated Instruction for teacher education students and future elementary educators.

Purpose

To provide an accessible resource for understanding how Differentiated Instruction is represented in BEED textbooks.

Audience

Designed primarily for BEED students, teacher educators, researchers, and future elementary teachers.

Approach

The portal organizes research findings using a clear, systematic, and educator-friendly structure.

Research Framework

Input – Process – Output

Input

Selected BEED textbooks and relevant instructional content.

Process

Systematic content analysis, coding, categorization, and comparison.

Output

Findings on the representation of Differentiated Instruction.

Implications

Information that may support textbook use, teacher preparation, and future research.
